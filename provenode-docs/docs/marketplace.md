# Pay-Per-Inference Marketplace

Monetize your AI models instantly using Shelby's native **Micropayment Channels**.

## Decentralized Monetization
Most decentralized storage protocols only support "Paid Storage." Shelby pioneers the **"Paid Read"** model. Provenode taps directly into this to build a seamless Pay-Per-Inference marketplace.

### The Flow
1. **Publish**: When you deploy a model in Provenode, you can list it on the Community Marketplace and set a price (e.g., `0.05 SBY per inference`).
2. **Import**: When another user or edge device attempts to pull your model, the Provenode SDK automatically negotiates a Shelby Micropayment Channel.
3. **Earnings**: You earn SBY tokens in real-time as your models are used across the DePIN network.

## Accessing the Marketplace
Navigate to **Growth -> Marketplace** to browse community models or publish your own with token-gated access.

## Multi-Chain Monetization (Solana Bridge)
While the Shelby Protocol coordinates on the Aptos blockchain, Provenode leverages Shelby's chain-agnostic data plane to offer **Multi-Chain Payments**.
When users click to import a premium AI model, they can choose to pay via the native **Aptos (SBY)** token, or use **Solana (USDC)**. Provenode's smart contract logic handles the bridging to seamlessly unlock the Shelby micropayment channel!
